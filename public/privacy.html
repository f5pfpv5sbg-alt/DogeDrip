Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Who we are

DogeDrip operates a cryptocurrency rewards platform. For privacy questions or requests, contact support.dogedrip@gmail.com.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Account details such as email address, username, account identifier and email-verification status.
  • Platform activity such as claims, reward balances, referrals, wheel activity, subscription status and support requests.
  • Withdrawal information you submit, including wallet address and requested amount.
  • Technical and security information such as IP address, browser/device details, request logs and anti-abuse verification results.
  • Information stored locally in your browser, such as your display preferences and referral attribution.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts, operate the platform, calculate and record rewards, process eligible withdrawals, prevent fraud and abuse, protect our services, respond to support requests, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Service providers and third parties

DogeDrip uses service providers to operate its services. These may include Firebase for authentication, Cloudflare for hosting, security and database infrastructure, and payment or wallet providers when payment features are enabled. These providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy practices.

If CPX Research surveys are enabled, users may be directed to or interact with CPX Research. CPX Research may collect survey, eligibility and related information under its own privacy policy. Review the provider's privacy terms before participating. DogeDrip does not control the provider's independent data collection.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share the minimum information necessary with service providers that help operate DogeDrip, when you request a third-party feature, when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the platform and our legal rights.

6. Cookies and local storage

Our website may use browser storage and similar technologies for essential functions, security, preferences and referral attribution. You can manage or clear browser storage through your browser settings, although some features may then stop working correctly.

7. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate your account, maintain reward and transaction records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes and meet legal requirements. We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to identity checks and applicable legal or operational retention requirements. You may also stop using the platform and clear local browser storage. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or to prevent fraud and resolve transactions.

9. Children's privacy

DogeDrip is intended for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly invite anyone under 18 to create an account or use reward features. If you believe a minor has provided personal information, contact us.

10. International processing and policy updates

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. We may update this policy as the platform changes. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support.dogedrip@gmail.com.
